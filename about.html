ABOUT US

Our story

The Yabatech Scrabble Club began in 2019 as a lunchtime gathering of students who wanted somewhere serious to play — and grew into a standing fixture of campus life at Yaba College of Technology. Today the club is open to students, staff, alumni, and members of the wider Lagos Scrabble community.

Our mission

Serious play, open door

To give every member — beginner or tournament veteran — a place to play competitive-standard Scrabble, improve deliberately, and represent Yabatech well beyond campus.

Affiliation

Yaba College of Technology

The club operates under YABATECH's student and community activities umbrella, using campus facilities for weekly meetings and larger inter-club events.

How we play

Word list

CSW / NWL friendly

Casual games are open to either word list — competitive/rated games use a club-agreed standard.

Timing

Clocked games

Most rated games run on a 25-minute clock per side, kept with a shared timer or the LexiClock app.

Ratings

Club-internal system

Ratings are calculated from logged club results and updated after each meeting.
